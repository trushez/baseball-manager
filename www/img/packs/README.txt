Team packs
==========

Put your own pictures here, one folder per club, named by the club's code (BOS, NYY, LAD, SEA and so on).
Inside, name each picture after the screen it goes behind:

  club.jpg      the club screen
  squad.jpg     the squad screen
  profile.jpg   player profiles
  tactics.jpg   batting and pitching selection
  matchday.jpg  match day
  match.jpg     the match itself
  news.jpg      your news
  stats.jpg     stats, league and fixtures
  default.jpg   anything else

Any you leave out fall back to default.jpg, then to the historical photos the game ships with.
Example: packs/BOS/club.jpg puts that picture behind the Boston club screen.
JPG or PNG both work (name PNGs .png). Large pictures are fine; they are shown darkened behind the text.
